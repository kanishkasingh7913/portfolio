I'm Kanishka — based in Bhopal, recently back from two years in Bengaluru where I built my first products and figured out what kind of work I actually want to do.

My path into product is unconventional. I studied Urban and Regional Planning — a degree that turned out to be surprisingly good training for product thinking. City planning is fundamentally about designing systems for people at scale, managing competing constraints, and making decisions with incomplete information. Turns out, so is product.

I started as a designer and gradually moved into PM work. I still care deeply about the craft side — the wireframes, the flows, the moment when a screen finally clicks into place. But what keeps me going is the earlier part: sitting with a messy problem, figuring out what's actually broken, and working backwards to something that makes sense for real people.
